## Abstract

Noise Guided Splatting studies the false transparency artifact in 3D Gaussian Splatting. The method injects opaque noise Gaussians into object volumes during training, encouraging surface Gaussians to learn higher opacity and produce cleaner geometry-aware reconstructions.
